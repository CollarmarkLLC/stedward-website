## From The Font

_“Be doers of the word and not hearers only”_

No amount of hard work, good deeds, kind words, or charitable giving is going to get any of us to Heaven. Salvation comes through Faith in Christ Jesus. BUT… What IS Faith in Christ Jesus? That really is the crux of the whole thing. As with all of religion, we have to be careful about imposing our own definitions and assumptions upon the Lord.

For the Jews, Faith was entirely a function of action. As we read in Deuteronomy: “hear the statutes and decrees which I am teaching you to observe, that you may live.” A good Jew did what God commanded. Period. It’s the same with Islam actually, the word “Islam” means obedience.

Because Judaism was a religion of external action rather than internal disposition, and because they were the Chosen People, it’s logical that Christianity would retain some aspects of that way of thinking. Christianity isn’t the opposite of Judaism, it’s a fulfilment of Judaism. So it is that Jesus says we must be doers AND we must love the Lord within our hearts. The two aren’t opposed to each other, they complement one another. Which brings us back to the question I posed: what is Faith in Christ Jesus? Simply put, Faith, in the Biblical sense, is a holistic (mind, body, spirit\soul\heart, will) configuration of the self to the worship, love, and teaching of God as He has revealed Himself in the person of Jesus Christ. And so Faith includes the intellect and the outer actions and the passionate heart and the deliberate freewill and all that goes with it.

One of the most common ways we see this explained in popular religious talk is that the Christian Faith is Both\And - meaning that we must be doers AND hearers, prayerful and charitable, forgiving and aware of our own sinfulness, etc. Rather than think in terms of yes or no questions, we do better to recognize that God tends to bring together ideas which seem like opposites or paradoxes… God, Himself, after all, is Three Divine Persons in one, singular and indivisible Godhead! Jesus is fully Human and fully Divine. There are limits to the way our minds can comprehend and while most human judgement is separating things into categories, the Lord tends to be bigger than that.

## Thoughts from Fr. Ryan

This Tuesday is a bit infamous in history in that the two worst Roman emperors share August 31st as their birthday. Caligula (born AD 12) and Commodus (born AD 192) were both given nearly absolute authority as very young men after being placed in the custody of those who believed that they could run the Empire soundly from behind the scenes… Hindsight is, of course, 20/20, but both of these disastrous emperors function as good reminders of how quickly the government can turn from friend to foe. US President Ronald Raegan, known for his quick wit and turn of phrase, famously said “The most terrifying words in the English language are: 'I'm from the government and I'm here to help.'”

After more than a century of hard, clear, tragic evidence of just how dangerous large, centralized governments are, I’m astounded by the attitudes of so many of us nowadays. Why are we placing our trust in the government at any level? Why are any of us so daft as to believe even a single word uttered by the talking heads standing at pulpits with large circular seals who claim to bring to us a gospel of vaccines or policies or treaties or anything whatsoever… As one of my old professors said: “Some things are so stupid, you have to be educated to believe them.”

I don’t say this to indict any of us specifically or even to address any one issue. I say it because history and the study of history provide a much needed context that we, as Christians, need to keep in mind. There is nothing new under the sun and Human nature never changes… Power corrupts and all men long for power over others...

Priests were murdered in the street in 17th Century England, 18th c France, 20th c Germany, Russia, Mexico, etc… The excuses changed a little, but the fact is that true religion is a threat to human power and the powerful always want to find a way to suppress the Church of which we are sons and daughters.

Daniel Ortega, for example, found a priest willing to sell his soul for power and brought the Jesuit Fr. Cardenal into his government as a “religious advisor.” He helped create an alternate version of Christianity using so-called ‘Liberation Theology’ to take over and ultimately destroy the religious faith of the people. (Pope St. John Paul II actually swatted Cardenal in the face when he met him!)

“Wokeness” and “Virtue Signaling” among Catholics use the same techniques as Fr. Cardenal. Every time I see a Catholic claiming that getting the vaccine is an “act of love” or that accepting someone’s “chosen gender” is what Jesus would do, I get a little sick...

Both are untrue and deceptive at best. This way of thinking is a cancer in the Church and history shows that it has always spelled death for the Church wherever it metastasizes…

Still, Jesus Christ is Lord, now and forever. When the Church dies somewhere, the Lord resurrects Her, and even those who suffer greatly as She dies here or there are rewarded with abundant graces and aids to salvation. And that’s an incredible blessing which the saints would tell us to be eager to gain! So let us pray for one another, seek to learn from history, and fight against dishonesty and the deceit of wokeness.
