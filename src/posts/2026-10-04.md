## Thoughts from Fr. Ryan

Wednesday is the Feast day of Our Lady of the Holy Rosary, AKA Our Lady of Victory. It celebrates a naval battle in 1571 in the Bay of Lepanto between Greece and Turkey. The battle was a last ditch effort. For 500 years, Christian forces had been fighting back against Muslim invasions in what we call Europe... The Christians had burned up all their resources fighting this war. A last ditch effort led by John of Austria was put together to prevent a new Muslim invasion into Greece. (The modern Muslim invasion is not an innovation, it’s living proof that those who ignore history are doomed to repeat it…)

The Christians were significantly outnumbered and the natural wind patterns gave the huge Muslim fleet the natural advantage. John of Austria brought everything he could, even the Pope. The battle started badly for us. Around midday, the Pope saw an image of the Blessed Virgin Mary in the clouds and he promised to establish a feast day if the Muslims were repelled. Well, the wind shifted unexpectedly and the Muslim fleet was cast into massive disarray. The Muslims lost most of the ships and enough wealth and supplies that they were unable to launch any of their planned ground attacks into Europe in the following years. Basically, today celebrates the end of the Muslim efforts to conquer Europe. And so it’s celebrated as the Feast of Our Lady of Victory or the Feast of Lady of the Holy Rosary.

And it’s providential that it coincides with our Gospel for today because there’s significant overlap between them. In the Gospel, Jesus describes servants who think that they’ve got it all figured out. They think they’ve got the upper hand. But Jesus chides them and says that they’re ability to recognize reality is warped. He sums it up by saying that the stone which the builders rejected has become the cornerstone - put another way, these professional builders misjudged the quality of the stone to their detriment. Put yet another way, the Jewish leadership didn’t recognize Jesus as the Messiah.

In our world, too, there are plenty of us - Christians, Catholics, Clergy included - who have lost the ability to recognize Jesus. It’s easy enough to find examples of Christians, Catholics, and clergy who think that they’re clever, but they can’t seem to find Jesus.

In their defense, the modern world is LOUSY with people and things pretending to be Jesus or Jesus-like.

And it can be legitimately difficult to sort out truth from fiction. Which brings us back to the Blessed Virgin Mary and the Rosary. Mary is like a COMPASS which always POINTS TO JESUS. The Rosary is THE MOST IMPORTANT PRAYER - other than the sacraments - that we have! If you’re struggling with doubts, pray the Rosary! If you’re not sure what to believe, pray the Rosary! If you’re concerned about the leadership in our Church, pray the Rosary! If you’re stuck in a habitual sin, dealing with an illness, depressed, anxious, unsure, WHATEVER, pray the Rosary!

Mary is not part of our faith by accident. She hasn’t been appearing in Europe for the past 200 years randomly. Her purpose is to POINT TO JESUS. It can seem COUNTERINTUITIVE, but it’s no accident that bad clergy never talk about Mary. It’s no accident that goofball priests and wacko theologians hate mention of Fatima and Lourdes and Garabandal. It’s no accident that the disasters within the Church are always associated with distance from Mary and the Rosary.

While she was alive, the Apostles always deferred to her. They went to visit her as much as they went to visit Peter. Our Lady is a profoundly important part of our Faith. I’d go so far as to say that it’s not really possible to be a good Catholic without devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary.

So if you’re already a saint, then you do you. Otherwise, on this feast day of our Lady, don’t trust yourself to identify the cornerstone from the counterfeit. Trust that Mary ALWAYS POINT TO JESUS! Pray the Rosary and ask Mary to show you to Jesus!
